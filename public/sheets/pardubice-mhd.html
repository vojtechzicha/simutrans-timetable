What this sheet models

Weekday afternoon peak (14:00–17:00, departures from the origin terminal) on an ordinary school day, taken from the national timetable feed (CIS JŘ NeTEx, export 23. 9. 2026) for Tuesday 6. 10. 2026, the first plain weekday after the 1. 10. change on line 29. Lines that don't run in the afternoon peak use the morning peak (06:00–09:00) instead. Night lines and school-only lines are left out and listed under Specials. Airport line 90 is included, although it runs only to meet flights.

“Every 10 min · :07 :17 …” is the headway and the minute offsets of departures from that terminal. Where the peak is not a clean clock pattern the real departures are listed. Travel times in the stop strips are minutes from the first stop, taken from a trip in the middle of the peak.

Stops and their lines

Every stop served in the modelled period. The numbers are trips in the 3-hour peak window heading to each destination (divide by 3 for trips per hour). Depot runs are left out.

Terminals

Stops where peak trips start. For each line: departures per hour, headway, and the actual departure minutes in the peak window. Arrivals of trips that end here are counted underneath.

Specials, connections and closures

Through-running, guaranteed connections, battery operation, long-term closures, and the lines left out of the model.

Fleet and vehicle allocation

What each line normally gets. The vehicles-seen count is from a real weekday allocation (Friday 25. 9. 2026). It shows the peak fleet on the line and is a sanity check for Simutrans convoy counts.
