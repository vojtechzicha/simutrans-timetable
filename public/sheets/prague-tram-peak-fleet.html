How this was built

  • Changes (13 Oct 2026). The closure comes from the PID notice (pid.cz). Routes and minutes come from PID GTFS export 2026-10-13, service date 20261020 (Tuesday 20 October: inside the long-term closure, after the short Na Knížecí – Smíchovské nádraží closure ends on 18 October). Each line's routes, schedule and trams needed at 16:00 are compared with the 27 September sheet; lines where only the order of the routes differs count as unchanged.
  • Fewest moves in the game. Nothing is re-rostered from new records: the trams are the 27 September set. A line that needs fewer trams gives up the surplus; a line that needs more takes freed trams, matched by type and depot to what ran on Monday 12 October (seznam-autobusu.cz). Trams left over go back to the depot. Lines 9 and 10 keep their own trams as the two circles, so only 7 trams join line 10 and 8 leave line 9.
  • Lines 9 and 10. In the GTFS every 9 arriving at Nákladové nádraží Žižkov leaves as 10 in the same minute and the other way round (block pairs, 0 min). Each direction of travel is a circle Sídliště Řepy → Nákladové nádraží Žižkov → Sídliště Řepy of 97–98 min, at a 4-minute interval: 25 trams each, 50 in all (the old 9 and 10 had 33 + 18 = 51). Departures from Hotel Golf are pull-outs from Vozovna Motol filling a Řepy slot, so they are not modelled.
  • Network and day. The network is the one in force on Sunday 27 September 2026. Vehicles come from the last weekday before it, Friday 25 September; Sunday has no weekday peak and only half the courses are recorded. Between the two days line 2 returned from the short Albertov diversion to its long route (26 Sep); line 23 had already switched to Královka – Bílá labuť on 19 Sep.
  • Resizing for the long line 2. On 25 Sep line 2 ran courses 1–10 to Albertov. On the long route (9–11 Sep) it ran courses 1–12: 2/11 is a Strašnice Tatra T3 pair and 2/12 a Pankrác Škoda 15T course. 2/11 uses 8779 + 8442, the set that ran it on 11 Sep and was free on 25 Sep. 2/12 uses the stand-in 9229, a Pankrác 15T3 free on 25 Sep, because its usual cars were busy elsewhere that day. Line 23 kept its 4 courses on the new route, so it is unchanged.
  • Designed trams. Where the records never show enough trams, the missing ones are designed (user instruction, 28 Sep): lines 13 (only course 1 ever recorded) and 31 (only courses 1–3). The number needed is the round trip at 16:00 (runtime both ways plus the scheduled turnaround at each end) divided by the headway. The added cars are single Tatra T3R.PLF cars that ran the line on other days of the last two weeks and were free on 25 Sep. They are marked “designed”.
  • Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz, vypravenost/pid/vypis?datum=2026-09-25&linka=N for each tram line: 506 records by tram-bus.cz, each with course, vehicle and a note. “ranní část / odpolední část” splits a course into its morning and afternoon vehicle; “náhrada za / nahrazen vlakem” records a swap (no time given; all vehicles are shown in order and the last one is counted); “přejezd na linku 6/7” records a move to another line. Lines 5 and 6 are one pool of line-5 courses that run through each other at Nádraží Holešovice. Course 22/42 moves to line 7.
  • Depots and types. Every car is matched to its home depot, type and paint in the seznam-autobusu.cz fleet register for DPP (823 trams in Hloubětín, Kobylisy, Motol, Pankrác, Strašnice, Vokovice, Žižkov and the Střešovice museum).
  • Simutrans schedule. Values for the Scheduling tab of your fork (calendar on). The fit follows the shared rule in pipeline/README.md: the longest exact run of one Departure every / Offset / Also at pattern (at most 8 slots, whole minutes, interval dividing 1440) around the 15:30–16:30 core, clipped to the pattern's first and last departure; among fits covering at least 80 % of the best, the simplest wins. The Fit column gives the run and lists where the 15:00–17:59 timetable differs. Loop termini are set 1 minute early: Offset and Also at are the timetable minute minus 1 (modulo the interval), with the real minute printed under each; stubs (Libuš, Pankrác), the Zvonařka triangle, the Nádraží Holešovice through stop and the Bílá labuť street stop keep the timetable minute. Depot runs are not modelled. A short working gets its own Simutrans line only if it runs in the 16:00 hour; the ones that end before the peak (3 from Nádraží Braník, 9 from Hotel Golf, 22 from Nad Džbánem) are listed as not modelled.
  • Pak128 objects. VZ-DPPraha-tram.pak, pak128 main 340c936, confirmed with the pak128 session. The object is chosen from each car's type and paint. Advert wraps get the nearest standard livery (marked ≈). All Tatra T3 variants couple with each other in any livery. Museum cars are the historic VZ-DPPraha objects; the two pre-war sets on line 42 use the historic Ringhoffer (Trabuko) motor car and trailer as the nearest match.
  • Routes. PID GTFS (data.pid.cz/PID_GTFS.zip, export 2026-10-13), service date 20261020 (for the 27 September sheet: export 2026-09-28, service date 20260929). Alighting and boarding stops of a loop are merged (Kobylisy → Březiněveská, Sídliště Modřany → Levského).
  • Not needed (user, 29 Sep 2026). Where more units are logged on a Simutrans line (a line, a branch, or the 5 + 6 pool) than it needs at 16:00, exactly the need is counted. One unit of every type on the line is kept first (most frequent type first), then recorded before added or designed, then the lowest course numbers. The rest are marked “not needed”, dimmed, and left out of the counts, depots and pak totals. This replaces the earlier practice of counting every logged unit as the operator's slack.
  • Ferries. PID lines of route type 4 in the same GTFS and service date. One row per direction: the interval in 16:00–16:59, the crossing time (median), the first and last departure of the day, and the Simutrans fit by the shared rule (piers are stub termini, so no loop shift). Boats needed = cycle_need with 0 minutes at the pier, because the timetable itself turns the boat round in the minute it lands. Operators from route_sub_agencies.txt.
  • Metro. Lines A, B and C from the same PID GTFS (route type 1). Interval = the gaps between departures in 16:00–16:59; line A alternates between Depo Hostivař and Skalka, so every other train turns at Skalka. Trips from or to the Kačerov depot on C are depot runs and are not counted. Train types: 81-71M on A and B, M1 on C, all five-car sets.
  • Rebuilding. This page is generated by pipeline/praha (python scripts/run.py --state 2026-10-12) from the raw data stored there; see pipeline/praha/README.md for how to update it when the timetable changes.
