Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the two terminus entries of each line. Units are calendar minutes. The offset is minutes after midnight modulo the interval. Leave up to stays automatic (half the gap). Loop terminus: every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure, so the tram clears the loop on time. Offset and each Also at value are the real minute − 1 (mod the interval; the interval is unchanged). The real minutes are shown underneath. Stub termini (Mifkova, Nemocnice Bohunice) keep the real minute. Put the timetable on the entry the tram leaves from: where a loop has a separate alighting stop set to All off, use the boarding (departure) entry. Each pattern is fitted to the afternoon peak core and reproduces the real departures exactly over the span shown. Line 12 uses a 14-min interval; 14 does not divide 1440, so the fork restarts the pattern at midnight, which does not affect the day. Short workings are separate lines, because Also at cannot change the route. Lines do not coordinate slots with each other, so their offsets are chosen to interleave. Depot runs are not modelled.

Method and sources

Vehicles. BMHD.cz vypravenost for 25 Sep 2026, lines 1–12, one page per line (line 1). The records come mostly automatically from DPMB/KORDIS vehicle positions. Ráno and ranní kurz mark morning-only duties. Odpoledne marks the afternoon car, and ráno i odpoledne a split duty that works both peaks.

Swaps. The notes před výměnou and po výměně carry no time. Swaps settled by where the cars turned up later: 1/11 and 9/6. For the others the sheet lists the replacement car and names the replaced one in the note.

Types and depots. BMHD fleet register (evidence DPMB), exact subtype, depot and livery as read on 28 Sep 2026. Lengths for the model: 32 m: Škoda 45T, 31 m: Škoda 13T, 30 m: Pars Nova K3R-N, ČKD Tatra KT8D5N, ČKD Tatra KT8D5R.N2, 22 m: Pragoimex VarioLF2R.E, 21 m: Pragoimex EVO 2, 20 m: Škoda 03T6, 15 m per car for Pragoimex T3R.EV, Pragoimex T3R.PV, Pragoimex VarioLFR.E, ČKD DS T6A5, ČKD Tatra T3G, ČKD Tatra T3P, ČKD Tatra T6A5.

Timetable. IDS JMK GTFS (kordis-jmk.cz/gtfs/gtfs.zip, stored 28 Sep 2026), tram trips of service day Fri 2 Oct. Trams per line: the minimum by runtime, and the number a tram followed through its round trip at 16:00 needs (timetable cycle, at least 3 min layover at each end).

Simutrans schedule. Fitted to the afternoon peak in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields (shared rule in pipeline/README.md). Loop terminus: set 1 min before the timetable departure so the tram clears the loop on time; stub termini (Mifkova, Nemocnice Bohunice) keep the real minute.

Cross-check. The kurz counts per line (24, 15, 19, 16, 13, 17, 9, 24, 17, 5, 14) on Fri 25 Sep equal the afternoon totals from the 8–10 Sep records in the Termini sheet. They also fit the GTFS, which has 22, 11, 14, 14, 8, 14, 6, 20, 13, 3 and 10 trips on the move at 16:00; the remaining trams are standing at termini.
