What the works are

  • Why: the Pražský okruh (D0) motorway ring is being built across the Praha – Benešov railway (KJŘ 221) near Říčany. For the construction, Správa železnic moved the railway onto a temporary alignment (provizorní přeložka) around the building site; that switch was the previous closure, 1 – 14 November 2025, with the same pattern of restrictions and the same XS9 bus. Now, about a year later, the line goes back from the temporary alignment to its original (final) position.
  • Where and what: between Praha-Uhříněves and Říčany (the stretch with the Praha-Kolovraty halt). Only one main-line track is in use; the other is being moved and connected. Trains in both directions share the one track, so they must wait for each other at Uhříněves or Říčany.
  • When: 11 October – 3 November 2026. The changed train timetable applies on workdays 12 – 16, 19 – 23, 26, 27, 29, 30 October and 2, 3 November, 5:20 – 21:30 (not 28 October, a holiday). Weekends and late evenings keep the normal timetable, with possible delays.
  • Effect on trains (ČD works timetable VJR-vlaky-221): the S9 trains numbered Os 91xx (the Milovice / Praha – Říčany / Strančice trains, about half of S9) do not run Praha-Uhříněves – Říčany – Strančice; they start and end at Uhříněves at their normal minutes. S9 to Benešov (Os 25xx) runs normally. R49 (Sp 2470 – 2476, 2479 – 2487) also stops at Strančice and Praha-Kolovraty, at its normal minutes. R 732, 730 and 728 (morning, towards Praha) also stop at Říčany. R17, Ex7 and the afternoon R49 from Tábor keep their timetable. ČD expects 5 – 10 min delays on the single track.
  • Replacement: bus XS9 Praha, Háje (metro C) – Uhříněves – Kolovraty – Říčany, every 10 / 15 / 30 min on workdays, PID and ČD tickets valid (modelled below).
  • Sources: PID notice, ČD works timetable (PDF), XS9 timetable (PDF), mhd86.cz, ČD press release on the 2025 closure.
