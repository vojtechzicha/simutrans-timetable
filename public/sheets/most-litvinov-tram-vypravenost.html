Lines

Headway, runtime and departure minutes are for the hour around each line’s peak moment. 16:00 is the shared reference hour. Line 1 (the Chempark shift line) has 5 trams at 15:00 but only 1 at 16:00; on 28 Sep 2026 the user decided to keep line 1 with that one tram and a designed frequency. Line 3 runs only in the morning and is shown at 06:00 (the rule for morning-only lines, confirmed by the user on 28 Sep 2026). User ruling of 28 Sep 2026: a short working gets its own Simutrans line only with at least 3 trips in 15:00–17:59, no depot at either end and at least one departure in the 16:00–16:59 hour; otherwise it is listed as not modelled, with no values. The count is the number of vehicle units at that moment; a coupled T3M.3 pair counts as one unit.

Tram counts. Every DPmML line runs between two termini at the peak, so each is one Simutrans line. Fits use the shared runfit rule around 16:00 (core 15:30–16:30); the morning-only line 3 uses 05:30–06:30. Line 1's schedule is designed (see schedule_override). Each line card shows how many trams are assigned and how many the line needs. “Needed” follows one tram round the line at its peak moment, with 3 min layovers or the terminus’s own shorter timetabled turnaround. “Minimum by runtime” is the two running times divided by the headway. Shared pools: None. Each line keeps its own trams at the peak.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. Each branch is its own Simutrans line. Each row is the longest stretch around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (shown as “exact from–to”). Among stretches of similar length the simplest pattern wins, and intervals that divide 60 come first. Loop departures one minute early: every DPmML terminus is a loop (smyčka), so each Offset and “Also at” value is set 1 min before the timetable departure, and the real minutes are listed under each entry. If a loop is built with a separate all-off arrival stop and a boarding stop, put the timetable on the boarding entry. “Leave up to” stays on automatic.

How this was built

The roster is designed, not observed. Nobody publishes which DPmML car ran which turn. seznam-autobusu.cz has no allocation records for any DPmML tram. The city's live vehicle map (mapy.mesto-most.cz, “Poloha vozidel MHD”) returned an empty layer, and dpmost.cz shows no vehicle positions. The trams were therefore placed by the operator's own rules and the timetable, as below.

How many trams. CIS JŘ timetable (JDF export of 25 Sep 2026, lines 575001–575004, versions valid from 14 Jun 2026), evaluated for Friday 25 Sep 2026. Trips were cut at Záluží, Chempark, where the trams now turn. They were then chained into vehicle turns at their termini (layover up to 40 min), and the turns running at the peak moment were counted.

Which trams.

  • Line 4: EVO2 were bought for line 4; 2 of its 4 turns carry a low-floor guarantee on every trip; 9 EVO2 are active (320–328). The newest, delivered in 2026, were picked. Cars: 325, 326, 327, 328.
  • Line 2: VarioLF plus 314 and 315 are registered as used on line 2 only; the guaranteed turn and one other get them, the third turn VarioLFR.S 253. Cars: 314, 315, 253.
  • Line 1: Kept with one tram (user decision 28 Sep 2026): the fixed T3M.3 pair 236 + 237, one of the five pairs the register lists as coupled sets. The shift runs before 16:00 (5 pairs at 15:00) are not in the peak and are not modelled. Cars: 236 + 237.
  • Line 3: Morning-only line, no guarantee: three solo T3M.3 in the standard livery. Cars: 302, 305, 308.

Only cars the register lists as active are used. In the workshops: 311 (Tatra T3M.3), 329 (Pragoimex EVO2), 330 (Pragoimex EVO2).

pak128 objects. Matched with the pak128 session to VZ-DPMostLitvinov-tram.pak. All five DPmML types have an exact object. Advertising-wrapped cars appear in the standard yellow-red livery, the only one in the pak.

Sources: CIS JŘ JDF, DPmML fleet register, DPmML: modernisation closure from 1 Mar 2026, DPmML changes, Most live map.
