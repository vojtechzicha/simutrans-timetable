Lines

Who is on which line. DPO works its trolleybuses in cross-line duties: a course of the 1xx group runs on 101, 106 and 107 in turn, the 2xx group on 102, 103, 105, 108 and 109, and the articulated 8xx group on 104 with a few 101 trips. Seznam-autobusu records such moves as 'přejezd na linku …' (moves to line …). Simutrans cannot change a vehicle's line, so each bus is placed on the line it was working at the peak, which is the line of its first live sighting between 16:29 and 16:50.

Branches and counts. Lines 101 and 107 each have a branch under the wire to Hranečník and a battery branch beyond it (101 to Radvanice, Ludvíkova every 30 min; 107 to Bartovice, Ještěrka every 20 min). The branches have different termini and different headways, and a route cannot change with 'Also at', so each branch is its own Simutrans line (A = Hranečník, B = battery branch). The real minutes are kept, so they interleave on the shared section as in the timetable. A bus seen east of Hranečník between 16:29 and 17:15 is on the battery branch. Every Simutrans line shows how many trolleybuses were seen and how many it needs. “Needed” follows one bus round the branch at 16:00, with 3 min layovers or the terminus's own shorter timetabled turnaround. A round trip that would wait over 30 min for a return trip is skipped, because by then the buses leave as another line. “Minimum by runtime” is the two running times divided by the headway. Interlining: At the peak each bus stays on one line long enough to be placed, but the duties hand buses between lines at shared termini: 107 to Hranečník ends at 16:33 and those buses continue as 101, and 105 buses continue as 103 at Koblov. The depot table shows each bus's later lines. No Simutrans line spans two DPO lines.

Headway and runtime come from the timetable for 15:00–17:00. “Leaves 16:xx” lists that terminus's departure minutes in the 16 o'clock hour.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. “Departure every 20, Offset 3, Also at 13” gives :03 :13 :23 :33 :43 :53. Each row is the longest stretch of the afternoon around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (shown under the entry as “exact from–to”). Among stretches of similar length, the simplest pattern wins, and intervals that divide 60 come first. “Leave up to” stays on automatic, which is half the gap.

Loop departures one minute early. Every DPO trolleybus terminus is a turning loop (smyčka), so each Offset and “Also at” value is set 1 min before the timetable departure, so the bus clears the loop on time. That includes the two battery termini, Radvanice, Ludvíkova and Bartovice, Ještěrka. The timetable minutes are listed under each entry for reference, and the “Leaves 16:xx” column shows the real timetable. Where a loop has a separate all-off arrival stop and a boarding stop, put the timetable on the boarding (departure) entry. Other timing points keep their real minutes.

How this was built

The roster is observed. DPO's live feed (dashboard.dpo.cz, vehicleType “troll”) was polled every minute from 16:29 on Tuesday 29 Sep 2026, a normal school weekday. Each trolleybus's line and course come from its first sighting between 16:29 and 16:50. That is 49 trolleybuses, against a timetable need of 46. The feed has no history, which is why the vehicle day is today rather than Friday 25 Sep, the tram sheet's day. The timetable versions are the same on both days (valid 1 Sep – 12 Dec 2026), and its demand is flat from 15:00 to 17:00, so the count at 16:30 matches the need at 16:00. The sheet therefore labels the peak 16:30, the moment actually observed.

Why not the Friday records. seznam-autobusu.cz (fed by tram-bus.cz) holds 42 DPO trolleybus records for Fri 25 Sep and 38 for today. The records list each bus with its first line and the lines it moves to during the day (“přejezd na linky 101 a 107”), but not the time. That is not enough to say where a bus was at 16:00. They are shown in the depot table as a cross-check.

How many per line. CIS JŘ timetable (JDF export downloaded 29 Sep 2026, DPO lines 915101–915109; the night lines 111, 112 and 113 are left out), evaluated for Tuesday 29 Sep 2026. Each Simutrans line is sized by its own round trip at 16:00 (cycle_need, shared with the tram sheets). Where fewer buses were seen than the round trip needs, the gap is filled with a designed trolleybus (dashed border): 2 in all. Each is an active car not seen at the peak, battery-capable where the branch needs it, preferring cars recorded on the line. line 103: 3912 (covers the SOR TNB 12 type of the line roster; 1 weekday records on line 103, last 2026-03-20; not seen in the live feed on 2026-09-29); line 109: 3749 (covers the Škoda 36Tr T'CITY type of the line roster; 3 weekday records on line 109, last 2026-09-15; not seen in the live feed on 2026-09-29).

pak128 objects. Each trolleybus is matched to its object in VZ-DPOstrava-trolleybus. The battery 26Tr (3731–3740) use the 26TrSolaris_partial object, and the others use 26TrSolaris. The 36Tr object is a battery object. The pak has the 26Tr, 27Tr and 36Tr only in the turquoise livery (dpotyrkysova) and the Trollino 12 only in blue-yellow (dpomodrozluta). So the older blue-white-yellow 26Tr and 27Tr, and the advertising wraps, show in turquoise; the Pak128 objects table lists each case.

Simutrans schedule. The departure times entered at loop termini (smyčky) are 1 min earlier than the timetable, as the model requires. The real times are listed under each entry.
