Lines

Branches. Branches are found by rule, as on the Ostrava bus sheet. Within 15:00–18:00, each pair of termini a line serves is its own Simutrans line when it has at least 6 trips (about 3 each way; a loop line that starts and ends at one stop needs 3), a departure in the 16 o'clock hour and no garage at either end. The busiest pair always counts. Smaller pairs (extensions, short workings) are not separate lines, because “Also at” cannot change the route; their minutes are folded into the main row from the same terminus, and their buses are added to the main branch. Where a line has two Simutrans lines, they are lettered A and B by trips.

Buses needed. “Needed” follows one bus round the branch from a departure around 16:00, with 3 min layovers or the terminus's own shorter timetabled turnaround, and counts the departures until it is back. A loop line counts the same way at its one terminus. A round trip that would wait more than 30 min (or 1.5 headways) for its next trip is skipped. The line is then topped up to the most buses on the road at once in 16:00–16:59 (each trip holds its bus until arrival plus that layover), so extensions and short workings keep their buses. Lines with no complete round trip around 16:00 use the chained count, at least one bus.

Headway and runtime come from the timetable for 15:00–17:00. “Leaves 16:xx” lists that terminus's real departure minutes in the 16 o'clock hour.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. “Departure every 30, Offset 6” gives :06 :36. Each row is the longest stretch around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (“exact from–to”). Rows marked “not scheduled” are variants with too few trips; their minutes are folded into the main row from the same terminus. On sparse lines a terminus with no pattern keeps “Departure every 0” (no timetable) and lists its real departures.

Departures one minute early. Every terminus is set as a loop, including turning points, as agreed for the tram, trolleybus and bus sheets. Each Offset and “Also at” value is 1 min before the timetable departure, so the bus clears the terminus on time. The real minutes are listed under each entry.

How this was built

No logged vehicles. The vehicle day is Friday 25 Sep 2026, the day of the tram sheet. seznam-autobusu.cz logs JDF-numbered lines under its nationwide section (/vypravenost/cr), and its day overview for that Friday lists no 575xxx or 576xxx line. The per-line lists hold 29 records in all, nearly all by registration plate in September 2023; the plates are matched to fleet numbers through the register. The city's live map layer is empty (see the tram sheet), and there is no other source. The sheet is therefore designed, and says so on every bus.

Designed roster (shared rule, user 29 Sep 2026). A line's history is every bus with a record on it or a photo showing its number. Each bus belongs first to the line of its newest evidence. Lines are filled from the largest need down. Picks first cover every type in the line's history that the picked buses lack, then go by recency: first buses whose newest evidence is this line, then other free buses with history on it. Where the history runs out, the line takes free buses of its home garage and main type, those with no evidence anywhere first. No bus is used twice, and no bus was logged anywhere that day. History buses left over are shown as “not needed”, dimmed and not counted. Then every type and livery of the active fleet must be on a line at 16:00 (user rule, 29 Sep): a missing one takes the place of a designed bus of a fitting line (its own evidence line, else a line of its garage; articulated buses only where articulated buses run), preferring a bus of the same type, then a garage pick, then the oldest evidence; the displaced bus becomes “not needed”. This puts one weekend-only double-decker on its own line 16, marked “normally weekends only”.

Tram replacement buses. Since 1 Mar 2026 (23:15) the tram section Záluží, Chemopetrol – Litvínov, Citadela is closed for the modernisation of the Litvínov track, and replacement buses run it 'in full, copying the current tram timetables' (dpmost.cz/zvd2026001). It is a long-term closure, so it is modelled as it runs: the buses are one Simutrans line at the tram minutes. The trips are the Chempark – Citadela part of the tram trips of lines 1, 3 and 4 in the city export (CIS JŘ city JDF export of 25 Sep 2026 (stored by pipeline/most-litvinov on 28 Sep)), at the tram's minutes. The photos of 2026 show SOR NB 18 articulated buses (191, 193, 194, 196, 199), the Urbanway 99 and the NB 12 38 on it.
