Prague Trolleybus Peak Fleet

The Prague trolleybus network as of Monday 2 November 2026. Lines 51 and 53 are on the 2 November network (PID timetable of Wednesday 4 November): from that day they run every trip of bus lines 131 and 176. Lines 52, 58 and 59 did not change and stay as published for Sunday 27 September (PID timetable of Tuesday 29 September). It lists every trolleybus at the afternoon peak by line and by garage, with the battery (off-wire) sections of each route, the Simutrans schedule for every terminus, and the matching objects of the custom Czech pak128 set. Vehicles on 52, 58 and 59 are those that ran on Friday 25 September (seznam-autobusu.cz deployment records). Vehicles on 51 and 53 are from the PID live map of Tuesday 3 November, except the ones the 52 card already has. Companion to the Prague Tram Peak Fleet sheet, built the same way.

How this was built

  • Network and day. Chosen the same way as for the Prague tram sheet. The network is the one in force on Sunday 27 September 2026. The timetable is PID's for Tuesday 29 September, the first weekday on that network (28 September is a public holiday). The vehicles come from the last weekday before it, Friday 25 September. Lines 51 and 53 changed on Monday 2 November (PID permanent change “Posílení trolejbusů v Praze”: every trip of bus 131 runs as trolleybus 51, every trip of bus 176 as trolleybus 53). Only those two lines moved to the 2 November network; the others keep their 27 September cards and trolleybuses.
  • Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz, vypravenost/pid/vypis?datum=2026-09-25&linka=N for lines 51, 52, 53, 58, 59 and 275: 42 records, each with a course, a vehicle and a note. “náhrada za / nahrazen vozem” records a swap (no time given). Coverage is checked against every day since 14 September. Courses 52/9, 59/6 and 59/14 run on other weekdays but are missing on 25 September. The recorded vehicles still cover the round-trip need on every line, so no trolleybus is designed. Lines 51 and 53 have no deployment record after the change: seznam-autobusu.cz refuses every scripted request since 1 November. Their trolleybuses come from the PID live map (mapa.pid.cz), polled every minute on Tuesday 3 November by the PID regional sheets. The map gives each vehicle its course and type, but it drops vehicles for minutes at a time and showed 53 poorly (58 of its 230 trips). Trolleybuses the 52 card already has stay there, so each line takes the map’s other trolleybuses first, then the one it already has in the game, then free trolleybuses of its type seen in service that week (marked designed). Each car’s note gives what the map showed.
  • Garages and types. Every vehicle is matched to its garage, type and paint in the seznam-autobusu.cz register for DPP trolleybuses (trakce=trolejbus). The register has 57 vehicles: Klíčov (SOR TNS 18 for line 58), Řepy (Škoda-Solaris 24m, SOR TNS 12, Bozankaya SNG 12T and Škoda 36Tr) and the Střešovice museum. Cars 515, 516, 518 and 542–544 were delivered after the register was read (29 September); their type is from the live map (516 and 518: the map gives none, and every typed car of the 515–544 series is a Bozankaya SNG 12T).
  • Trolleybuses needed. Follow a trolleybus leaving around 16:00 round the line, taking the first departure at least 3 minutes after each arrival. The departures in that round trip are the number needed. A line per branch is counted on its own; a line over both branches is followed round all four of its entries.
  • Branches. Lines 52 and 58 each have two branches. A line runs as one Simutrans line over both branches where that needs fewer trolleybuses than one line per branch, and as one line per branch otherwise (owner, 7 November: “if it needs more, keep it as two lines”). 58 is one line (13 against 7 + 7); on the PID live map of Tuesday 3 November its trolleybuses run both branches. 52 is two lines (5 + 3; one line would need 9), although every 52 trolleybus alternates the branches in real life; its courses are split in course order, a choice. Each card says up front how many Simutrans lines it is.
  • Simutrans schedule. The values go on the Scheduling tab of your fork (calendar on). The fit follows the shared rule in pipeline/README.md: the longest exact run of one Departure every / Offset / Also at pattern, in whole minutes, with at most 8 slots. The core is the 16:00 reference hour, not 15:30–16:30: line 58's inbound 12-minute pattern starts at 16:20, and the earlier window gave a 40-minute pattern fitted to only four departures. Every terminus is set 1 minute early. A trolleybus cannot reverse. It arrives at one stand, turns by a loop, a bus-station circuit or a block, and leaves from another stand; block loops count as loops, as in the Prague tram sheet. Offset and Also at are the timetable minute minus 1 (modulo the interval), with the real minute printed under each. Put the values on the departure stand.
  • Battery sections. Each route's GTFS shape (PID shapes.txt) is sampled every 10 m and compared with the OpenStreetMap ways tagged trolley_wire=yes (Overpass, data of 2026-09-29, 25 m tolerance). A stop-to-stop section counts as off-wire when less than a third of it runs under the wire. OpenStreetMap is volunteer data, so a newly built or removed section of wire may be missing. The pak128 objects run with or without wire.
  • Pak128 objects. VZ-DPPraha-trolleybus.pak, pak128 main c6f4b44 (build/VZ-DPPraha-trolleybus in the pak checkout). Every peak type has its own object, in the PID grey-red livery, except 509, which wears DPP’s purple “90 years of Prague trolleybuses” paint and has its own object.
  • Routes. PID GTFS (data.pid.cz/PID_GTFS.zip, export 2026-09-28, route type 11 = trolleybus), service date 20260929; lines 51 and 53 from the export of 2026-11-02, service date 20261104. The airport terminus is one loop: trips end at Letiště and start at Terminál 1, so both are merged as Terminál 1.
  • Rebuilding. This page is generated by pipeline/praha-trolleybus (python scripts/run.py --state 2026-11-02) from the raw data stored there. pipeline/praha-trolleybus/README.md explains how to update it when the timetable changes.
