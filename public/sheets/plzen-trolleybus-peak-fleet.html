Battery sections

Stretches run off the wire, on the traction battery. The PMDP GTFS has no route shapes, so every stop of each stop pattern is tested against the OpenStreetMap overhead wire (trolley_wire, read 29 Sep 2026): a stop more than 60 m from any wire is off the wire, and consecutive such stops form a stretch, named from the last stop under the wire. Lengths are straight lines between stops, so the road distance is a little longer. Battery-capable trolleybuses (marked battery in the lists): every Škoda 26 TrNU and 27 TrNU, and the Škoda 26 Tr 565–582 (Wikipedia, Škoda 26Tr and 27Tr fleet lists). The 26 Tr 551–552 and 27 Tr 527–530 and 540 have an auxiliary diesel unit instead (marked diesel auxiliary). In the pak every trolleybus object is a road vehicle with electric traction, so the game needs no wire; the check below is what the real operation requires.

Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entries listed for each Simutrans line. Units are calendar minutes; the offset counts from 01:00, as the fork does: minutes after 01:00 modulo the interval (the same as after midnight for every interval that divides 60); Leave up to stays automatic. Every PMDP trolleybus terminus is a turning loop (as on the Brno, Prague and Ostrava trolleybus sheets), so every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure to clear the loop on time: offset and each Also at value are the real minute − 1 (mod the interval), with the real minutes shown underneath. Put the timetable on the entry the trolleybus leaves from. Branches that trolleybuses alternate between (10, 11) are one Simutrans line with the shared terminus twice; the 15 branch to Nová Hospoda is its own line. Each line is fitted at one rate, the number of 16:00–16:59 departures most of its entries share, so no end sends more trolleybuses than the other. Plzeň’s afternoon timetable is irregular (gaps of 5 to 9 min on 12 and 16, 12 and 24 on 10), so many entries cannot be exact: they are marked approximate with their largest deviation and the real 16:xx minutes. Depot runs are not modelled.

Method and sources

Which day. The network is the timetable in force on Tue 29 Sep 2026 (PMDP GTFS, service day 29 Sep; line 12 shifts by a minute from 1 Oct). The vehicles are from Fri 18 Sep 2026: seznam-autobusu.cz has one or no trolleybus record on 23, 24, 25 and 29 Sep, and 28 Sep was a holiday, so 18 Sep is the latest complete Friday (Mon 21 and Tue 22 Sep are complete too). The Plzeň tram sheet uses the same day.

Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost (mhd-plzen), one list per line, e.g. line 16 on 18 Sep. The site files 22 and 49 among the buses, but the cars logged on them are trolleybuses, and the GTFS marks them as trolleybus routes. Three cars logged on 18 with no note also worked 10, 12 and 13 that day; they are counted there.

Types, depots, liveries. seznam-autobusu.cz fleet register of PMDP (trolleybuses in service), read 29 Sep 2026. Car ids are the key, because PMDP trolleybus and bus numbers overlap. Battery capability: Wikipedia (Škoda 26Tr, Škoda 27Tr, Trolejbusová doprava v Plzni).

Battery sections. OpenStreetMap trolley_wire (Overpass, 29 Sep 2026) against the GTFS stops, cross-checked with Wikipedia’s list of partial-trolleybus stretches.

Simutrans schedule. Fitted in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields, using the shared rule in pipeline/README.md (core 15:30–16:30, longest exact run, simplest pattern, one rate per line). Line 10 is fitted every 24 min per branch, its real pattern, and 22 from Koterov every 30.

Rebuild. Generated by pipeline/plzen-trolleybus (state “2026-09-29”), which imports helpers from the Plzeň tram pipeline (pipeline/plzen) and the Brno trolleybus pipeline; see pipeline/plzen-trolleybus/README.md. The Plzeň bus sheet is built by the same scripts.
