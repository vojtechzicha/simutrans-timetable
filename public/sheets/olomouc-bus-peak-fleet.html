Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entries listed for each Simutrans line. Units are calendar minutes; the offset is minutes after midnight modulo the interval, and Leave up to stays automatic. Where the bus turns, every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure so it leaves the turning point on time (as on every bus and trolleybus sheet since 29 Sep): offset and Also at are the real minute − 1, with the real minutes underneath. Through stops (Nové Sady on 16) keep the real minute. Each Simutrans line is fitted at one rate (the 16:00–16:59 departures most of its entries share) so that no end sends more buses than the other; where an end runs a different number, the row is marked partial and lists the minutes left out or added. A “+” line under a stop names a variant whose departures are folded into that row.

Buses needed at 16:00: follow a bus leaving the first entry around 16:00 through every leg of its line, taking the first departure at least 3 min after arriving, or less where the timetable itself turns faster (the timetable cycle); a round trip that would wait longer than the line’s interval (at least 30 min) is skipped. As on the Prague, Ostrava and Brno bus sheets, a real line never gets fewer buses than the most it has on the road at once between 16:00 and 16:59 (each trip holding its bus until arrival plus the terminus’s shortest turnaround, at most 3 min); the difference goes to the line’s first Simutrans line.

Buses by line

One row per bus, grouped by Simutrans line. logged buses were recorded on the line on Tue 29 Sep 2026 (seznam-autobusu.cz). designed buses are not recorded that day: each is a real DPMO bus that ran the line on weekdays before (dates given), is in service, and was not logged anywhere on 29 Sep. The picks first cover every bus type of the line’s roster that the other buses lack, then take the buses that ran the line most recently. Rows marked not needed are dimmed at the bottom: logged buses beyond the line’s need (one of every type kept first; with no kurz numbers, the buses listed later on the record page go first), and free buses from the line history that the need does not use. They are left out of every count, the depot and the pak totals (user rule, 29 Sep). Every bus shows its number and garage, its full type and length, and the pak128 core.

Method and sources

Which day. Network: the DPMO timetable in force on Tue 29 Sep 2026. Buses: Tue 29 Sep 2026, the only September weekday with a useful number of DPMO bus records (Fri 25 Sep and Fri 18 Sep have none). See “Which day”.

Buses. seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost, MHD Olomouc (day overview), one page per line. The records name no kurz, no time and no note for buses.

Designed buses. The line histories since 1 Jan 2025 (all pages), weekdays only. Roster of a line: the logged types plus every type with at least two weekday records on it in 2026 (a single record is treated as a one-off substitute). A designed bus is in service in the register, was not in the 29 Sep day overview, and is used once. Order: recent history (2026), then 2025 history, then free buses of the line’s types; the picks first cover roster types the line lacks.

Types and garage. seznam-autobusu.cz fleet register of DPMO (iddopravce=134, buses, in service), read on 29 Sep 2026; 501 (SOR NS 12 electric) and 625 (Urbino 12 III, laid up long-term) are not in the listing and come from their vehicle pages.

Timetable. DPMO GTFS (dpmo.cz, version 17 Sep 2026, the file stored by the Olomouc tram pipeline; the current download is identical), bus routes (route_type 3), service day 29 Sep 2026. The feed has no vehicle blocks; the order of legs on 16 and the split of 11 come from chaining trips (scripts/propose.py).

Simutrans schedule. Shared fit rule (pipeline/README.md): the longest exact run of one Departure every / Offset / Also at pattern (at most 8 slots) around the peak core 15:30–16:30, one rate per Simutrans line; turning points −1 min.

Rebuild. Generated by pipeline/olomouc-bus (state “2026-09-29”), stdlib-only Python; see its README for every decision and the update recipe.
