Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entries listed for each Simutrans line. Units are calendar minutes; the offset is minutes after midnight, modulo the interval; Leave up to stays automatic. Every entry here is a terminus where the bus turns, so every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure to leave the turning point on time: offset and each Also at value are the real minute − 1 (mod the interval), with the real minutes shown underneath. A timing point the bus passed without turning would keep the real minute; there is none on these lines. Put the timetable on the entry the bus leaves from. Branches are separate lines (26 Nová Ves, 30 Borská pole, 41 Křimice); 28 alternates between its two Bukovec termini and is one line with CAN Husova twice; 33 and 40 form a loop run in both directions, one Simutrans line per direction. Each line is fitted at one rate, the number of 16:00–16:59 departures most of its entries share. Where the afternoon timetable is irregular, the entry is marked approximate with its largest deviation and the real 16:xx minutes. Depot runs are not modelled.

Buses needed at 16:00: follow a bus leaving the first entry around 16:00 through every leg of its line, taking at each terminus the first departure at least 3 min after arriving, or less where the timetable itself turns faster; a round trip waiting longer than the line’s interval (at least 30 min) is skipped. The need is never below the most buses on the road at once between 16:00 and 16:59 per PMDP record line (each trip holding its bus until arrival plus min(3 min, the terminus’s shortest timetabled turnaround)); the extra goes to the main line. Where more buses were logged than the need, exactly the need stays counted and the rest is marked not needed (dimmed, left out of every count, the garage and the pak totals): one of every type of the line first, then afternoon-only kurzy and the highest kurz numbers are dropped first (user rule, 29 Sep 2026; pipeline/brno-trolleybus common.mark_spares). Where fewer were logged, real cars from the line’s own history are designed in; on 18 Sep no bus line was short once 40 and 42 are taken with the kurzy they run on.

Method and sources

Which day. The network is the timetable in force on Tue 29 Sep 2026 (PMDP GTFS, service day 29 Sep). The buses are from Fri 18 Sep 2026: seznam-autobusu.cz has 0–4 bus records on 23, 24, 25 and 29 Sep, and 28 Sep was a holiday, so 18 Sep is the latest complete Friday (Mon 21 and Tue 22 Sep are complete too). The Plzeň tram and trolleybus sheets use the same day.

Buses. seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost (mhd-plzen), one list per line, e.g. line 30 on 18 Sep. 40 has no record since 31 Aug and 42 none since December 2024: their buses are taken from the 33 and 26 kurzy (see those cards). Line history for designed buses: vypis?linka=N&since=…, stored in pipeline/plzen-trolleybus/data/raw/history.

Types, garage, liveries. seznam-autobusu.cz fleet register of PMDP (buses in service), read 29 Sep 2026, keyed by the site’s car id.

Simutrans schedule. Fitted in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields, using the shared rule in pipeline/README.md (core 15:30–16:30, longest exact run, simplest pattern, one rate per line).

Rebuild. Generated by pipeline/plzen-bus (state “2026-09-29”), which runs the Plzeň trolleybus pipeline’s scripts (pipeline/plzen-trolleybus/scripts, with --base ../../plzen-bus) on the raw data stored there; see pipeline/plzen-bus/README.md.
