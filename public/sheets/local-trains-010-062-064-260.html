Simutrans design for the workday Os and Sp trains around Mladá Boleslav and Česká Třebová. Times come from the national CIS JŘ timetable JR2026 (workday, 6 October 2026). Long-distance trains (R, rj, EC, RegioJet, Arriva R22) are not included.

Notation: L = odd hours, S = even hours. "Offset" in the fork: odd hours come first, so offset 4 means every 2 h at L:04, and even hours add 60 (offset 66 means S:06). (x) = request stop. Highlighted rows are timed stops with a longer stay, which the train needs to keep to time.

Mladá Boleslav area

How the three lines share track:

  • Nymburk → Mladá Boleslav: 062 at L:04 and 064N at L:32. Both run in odd hours, so the gaps alternate between 28 min and 1 h 32 min. That's deliberate: the real timetable is uneven too.
  • Mladá Boleslav → Nymburk: from MB hl. n., 062 leaves at S:14 and 064N at S:41.
  • MB město → Mladějov: hourly at :31, 064 in odd hours and 064N in even hours.
  • Mladějov → Mladá Boleslav: hourly at :34, 064 in even hours and 064N in odd hours.

Česká Třebová area

How the four lines share track:

  • Kolín → Česká Třebová: hourly at :44, 010S in odd hours and 010 in even hours.
  • Česká Třebová → Kolín: hourly at :59, 010S in odd hours and 010 in even hours.
  • Česká Třebová → Svitavy: an Os every hour at :07 (010S in even hours, 260 Os in odd hours), plus the Sp at S:48.
  • Svitavy → Česká Třebová: an Os every hour at :38 (010S in odd hours, 260 Os in even hours), plus the Sp at S:59.
  • Svitavy – Letovice: only the 260 Os and the Sp, as in reality.

In reality the two Kolín – Svitavy pairs fill 260 Os slots in the early afternoon. Here they are their own line in the hours the 260 Os doesn't use, so both run all day without colliding.
