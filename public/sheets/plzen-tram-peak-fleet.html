Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the terminus entries of each line. Units are calendar minutes. The offset counts from 01:00, as the fork does: minutes after 01:00 modulo the interval (the same as after midnight for every interval that divides 60); every interval divides 1440, so the pattern runs unchanged all day. Leave up to stays automatic (half the gap). Loop terminus: every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure, so the tram clears the loop on time. The fork counts offsets from 01:00, so on an interval that does not divide 60 the typed value is (real − 60 − 1) mod the interval: the real minute is under each value. Every Plzeň terminus is a loop, so every offset is the real minute − 1 (mod the interval); the real minutes are shown underneath. Put the timetable on the entry the tram leaves from: where a loop has a separate alighting stop set to All off, use the boarding (departure) entry. Each pattern reproduces the real departures exactly over the span shown. Depot runs are not modelled.

Method and sources

Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost for MHD Plzeň, Fri 18 Sep 2026, lines 1, 2 and 4 (line 1). The site has no Plzeň tram records for 23, 24 or 25 Sep; Mon 21 and Tue 22 Sep are complete too and show the same kurz structure. Ráno marks a morning-only car, odpoledne the afternoon car, ráno i odpoledne a split duty, výměna za X a car replaced by X, and náhrada za Y the replacement.

Cross-check. Chaining the Fri 25 Sep timetable gives 14, 14 and 22 trams in service at 16:00 on lines 1, 2 and 4, against 14, 15 and 22 kurzy recorded on 18 Sep. One line 2 split duty is probably already back in the depot at 16:00.

Types, depot, livery. The seznam-autobusu register for PMDP trams, read on 28 Sep 2026. Lengths for the model are approximate: 15 m per car for T3R.PLF, VarioLFR.S and VarioLF+, about 20 m for EVO2, 40T and VarioLF2/2IN, and 30 m for KT8D5.RN2P.

Timetable. PMDP GTFS (jizdnirady.pmdp.cz/jr/gtfs), valid from 22 Sep 2026; trips running on Fri 25 Sep.

Simutrans schedule. Fitted to the afternoon peak in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields: the longest stretch that one pattern reproduces exactly, around 15:00–16:00 for lines 1 and 2 and from 16:00 for line 4. Loop terminus: set 1 min before the timetable departure so the tram clears the loop on time. Every Plzeň terminus is a loop, so every entry is the real minute − 1.

Pak objects. From the pak128 session: VZ-PMDP-tram.pak, pak128 main 6547eb6, all in PMDP yellow-grey-white (pmdpzlutosedobila). Every type in the peak has an exact object.
