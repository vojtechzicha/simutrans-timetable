How to read the line cards

Typing the timetable. Values go into the Scheduling tab of the terminal entry, with the calendar on: Departure every, Offset, Also at (at most 7). Offsets count from 01:00 and every terminal is set as a loop, so each value is (real departure − 61) mod interval; the real clock minute is under every value. A row with Every 0 has no timetable: the bus leaves on arrival.

Core points and knots. Each line was read over the whole operating day (5:00–23:00) at its core points: terminals, knots where variants split, shared hubs. A trip counts at the time it passes a core point, wherever it starts. Where a slot is defined at a knot, the terminal departure is the knot time minus the running time, and the knot gets no timetable.

Variants. Each destination, path or shift variant is its own Simutrans line on a longer interval (the save game's Ex4). Shift lines repeat every 480 (8 h), the longest interval allowed. Trips more than 3 min off every slot are off the pattern, in orange.

Buses needed. One bus followed round the line's own slots with the real running times and at least 3 min at each terminal. Real buses swap lines at the terminals, so per-line totals are higher than what the operators run; pooling lines is the owner's choice and is not done.

In the game. Each card compares the save's line (export of 30 Oct 2026) with the modelled stops. The save's buses are shown as a check; the sheet is built from the real timetable and the buses seen running, so a game line may need buses added or moved.

How this was built

Scope. PID GTFS downloaded Sun 1 Nov 2026 (feed 1-14 Nov). Service day Mon 2 Nov 2026 (the first live-map poll day), a normal school weekday (28 Oct was a holiday and 29-30 Oct autumn school holidays; regional lines run many school-day-only trips). The Středočeský kraj licenses each district's lines with its own prefix (27xxxx = okres Nymburk); a line licensed by Prague or by another kraj is on the sheet of the okres of its outer end (the first stop, coming in from the far end, in a Středočeský municipality), or, with no Prague end, of the okres most of its stops lie in. 31 lines: 31 modelled here, 0 on the Prague bus sheet, 0 listed with the reason.

Method. pipeline/bus-schedule-method.md (v4): whole-day reading at core points, repeating interval, Ex4 variants, timing at terminals worked back from the knots, no daily lines (at most every 480), every afternoon stop on a line, buses from the pattern, every line checked by hand (method-check file in the pipeline folder).

Buses. PID live map (mapa.pid.cz getData.php), polled every minute 12:00-20:00 (every 3 min 5:00-12:00 and 20:00-23:00) on Mon 2 Nov 2026, and on Tue 3 Nov 2026 so far only 5:00-7:20 (this version, 3 Nov morning: the Tuesday trips fill only designed buses and missing types; the evening version adds the whole Tuesday), by scripts/01_live_poll.py; each bus once per line through getVehicleWindow.php (operator, type, course). A bus fills one Simutrans line it ran in that line's window, by matching, across all twelve oblast sheets; buses on the Prague bus sheet are not used here.

Sources: PID GTFS, PID live map, the save-game export of 30 Oct 2026 (engine session).
