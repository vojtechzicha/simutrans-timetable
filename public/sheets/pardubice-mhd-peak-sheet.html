Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entry each vehicle leaves from. Units are calendar minutes, Offset counts from midnight modulo the interval, and Leave up to stays automatic. Every DPMP terminus is a turning loop or turning point (točna), so every departure is set 1 min before the timetable: Offset and Also at are the real minute − 1, with the real minutes underneath. Branches are separate lines. Every entry reproduces the whole 16:00–16:59 hour: an exact repeating pattern where one exists, otherwise the hour’s own minutes repeated hourly (many DPMP afternoon timetables are irregular). An entry with at most 8 departures a day gets Departure every 1440 with all of them (exact all day).

Method and sources

Which day. Network: the timetable in force on Tue 29 Sep 2026. Vehicles: Fri 25 Sep 2026, the last weekday before it with records (28 Sep was a holiday); one real day. The old sheet used Tue 6 Oct for the network because line 29 changes on 1 Oct; 29 is a school line and left out, so the modelled lines are the same.

Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost “MHD Pardubice” (data from tram-bus.cz) for Fri 25 Sep 2026: 104 records, 96 kurzy. ráno marks a morning duty, odpoledne the afternoon car of a split duty, nahrazen / náhrada za a car replaced during the day. Lines 16, 18, 22, 33, 88 and 90 have no weekday record on the day. Line histories since 1 Sep 2026 (and the latest records for lines with none) give the designed cars; the day overview checks that a designed car was not out that day.

Types, paint and depot. The DPMP register on seznam-autobusu.cz (DP města Pardubic, iddopravce 47, read 29 Sep 2026), matched by the site’s car id. Traction batteries from the old sheet (trolejbus.cz fleet list of 18 Mar 2026).

Timetable. CIS JŘ JDF 1.10, export of 28 Sep 2026 (the copies downloaded by the Ostrava pipelines; DPMP’s inner files are kept in pipeline/pardubice/data/raw/jdf). Version rule: among a line’s versions valid on the day, the latest “valid from” wins. DPMP marks its school-holiday trips with “runs in” date ranges; they do not run on 29 Sep.

Simutrans schedule. The shared rule (pipeline/README.md) with the Brno bus sheet’s requirement that every entry reproduces the 16:00 hour; all termini −1 min.

Earlier user decisions kept. Airport line 90 included; night lines 98 and 99, school line 29, the Škola Svítání service 910 and the cancelled bridge lines 902 and 906 left out; line 20 (ends 14:43) and line 90 modelled on their own times; long-term closures modelled as they run (the provisional Kapitána Bartoše bridge, line 13’s detour, the Tuněchody works), short-term ones ignored; no accessibility data.
