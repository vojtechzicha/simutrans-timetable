Branches and tram counts. The timetable does not say which end a tram serves next, so the trips were chained in arrival order at each terminus, in-service trips only (depot runs to Plynárny and Poruba, vozovna removed). On lines 8 and 17 every tram keeps to one end all afternoon. On 4 and 7 the ends mix irregularly (e.g. vozovna, vozovna, Vřesinská, Vřesinská), and line 4 sends 4 trams an hour to Hranečník against 2 to Nová huť jižní brána. None of them alternates strictly A–B–A–B, which is the only pattern one Simutrans line can model. So each branch is its own Simutrans line, sized by its own round trip. The real minutes are kept, so the branches interleave on the shared section as in the timetable. Under each line card, every Simutrans line shows how many trams are assigned and how many it needs. “Needed” follows one tram round the branch at 16:00, with 3 min layovers or the terminus’s own shorter timetabled turnaround. “Minimum by runtime” is the two running times divided by the headway. The trams from the timetable chaining are shared out so that every branch gets at least its need. Shared pools: None at the afternoon peak. Lines 1 and 2 share course numbers and swap trams at Hlavní nádraží at some time of day (weekday records say “přejezd na linku 1/2”; on the holiday of 28 Sep every tram alternated). But the weekday peak timetable lets each line turn there in 3 min, against 8 min for a swap. The rule is the shortest wait at the terminus, so at 16:00 every tram stays on its line and 1 and 2 are separate Simutrans lines (15 trams, not 16).

Headway and runtime come from the timetable for 15:00–17:00. “Leaves 16:xx” lists that terminus's departure minutes in the 16 o’clock hour. The count is the number of vehicle units working the line at 16:00; a coupled Vario pair counts as one unit.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. Each branch is its own Simutrans line. “Departure every 20, Offset 3, Also at 13” gives :03 :13 :23 :33 :43 :53. Each row is the longest stretch of the afternoon around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (shown under the entry as “exact from–to”). Among stretches of similar length, the simplest pattern wins, and intervals that divide 60 come first. “Leave up to” stays on automatic, which is half the gap.

Loop departures one minute early. Every Ostrava terminus is a loop (smyčka), so each Offset and “Also at” value is set 1 min before the timetable departure. That way the tram clears the loop on time. The timetable minutes are listed under each entry for reference, and the “Leaves 16:xx” column shows the real timetable. If a loop is built with a separate all-off arrival stop and a boarding stop, put the timetable on the boarding (departure) entry. Other timing points on the line keep their real minutes.

How this was built

Friday's roster is designed, not observed. No public record of which tram ran on which line on 25 Sep exists. The tram-bus.cz feed behind the seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost for ODIS stopped recording DPO trams on 26 Jun 2026, and on 25 Sep it carries only trolleybuses. DPO's live map shows current positions only. The roster was therefore built from the evidence below, so each number is a tram that realistically worked that line, not a verified sighting.

How many trams per line. CIS JŘ timetable (JDF export of 20 Oct 2026 (timetable versions valid on Wed 21 Oct 2026), DPO lines 915001–915017) evaluated for Friday 25 Sep 2026. Trips were chained into vehicle turns at their termini (layover up to 40 min), and the turns running at 16:00 were counted. Since the user's rule of 29 Sep, only each Simutrans line's round-trip need is counted. The extra units from the chaining are shown as “not needed” (one of each type kept first), a line missing a regular type of its history roster (at least 2 % of its records) gets one unit swapped for that type, and the free cars from each line's history are listed as “not needed”.

Which types. About 11,000 weekday vehicle records from seznam-autobusu.cz (Sep 2025 – Jun 2026, 2026 weighted double, records marked as afternoon runs weighted up, morning-only runs left out) give each line's type mix. That mix is capped by how many cars of each type are active today, keeping a small reserve. The 8 surviving T3R.P are not planned (four more were retired in 2026 and offered for sale on 17 Sep). Single Vario LF cars on lines 1 and 2 become coupled pairs, which is how they run there. Courses numbered 9xx are late-evening runs (out to the night lines and to 8 and 11) and are left out: the single Vario LF cars seen on 4, 7 and 8 run only on those, so they are not planned at 16:00. A coupled pair stays a pair on every line. On 18 Oct 2026 five trams of the earlier sheet were replaced for this reason, each marked on its line; every other tram stays as published.

Which trams. Only cars the fleet register lists as active (not in the workshops or out of service) are used. The exception is a car that was seen running on Mon 28 Sep, which overrides a stale register entry. Cars are ranked per line by their records on that line, whether they ran on that line on Mon 28 Sep (DPO live feed, 53 trams seen), and recent activity. Coupled pairs reuse pairs seen in the records.

pak128 objects. Each tram is matched to its object in VZ-DPOstrava-tram.pak (custom Czech pak128, main 4fbb7b8), the only tram pak installed. The livery follows the car's registered paint: blue with white stripe uses dpotyrkysova, blue-white-yellow uses dpomodrozluta, and silver-gold uses stribrna. Advertising wraps use the type's default livery and are listed as nearest in the Pak128 objects table. VarioLFR.E, VarioLFR.S and T3R.EVN look the same as the Vario LF, and VarioLF2R.S the same as the Vario LF2, so the pak names them as variants of those objects.

Simutrans schedule. The departure times entered at loop termini (smyčky) are 1 min earlier than the timetable, as the model requires. The real times are listed under each entry.

Depots. Taken from each car's current depot in the fleet register: Poruba and Moravská Ostrava (Křižíkova).

Sources: CIS JŘ JDF, seznam-autobusu.cz vypravenost ODIS, DPO fleet register, DPO live dashboard, DPO výluky, mhd-ostrava.cz.
