What this is

Each line is a set of trains sharing one corridor. Each variant is one distinct path (different terminus or tail) and gets its own Simutrans schedule with its stopping pattern, depot visit(s) and waiting spots. Headways are taken from the afternoon peak (13–20 h) where a variant runs then, otherwise from the whole day.
