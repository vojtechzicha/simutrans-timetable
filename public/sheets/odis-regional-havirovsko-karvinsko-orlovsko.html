How to read the line cards

Typing the timetable. Values go into the Scheduling tab of the terminal entry, with the calendar on: Departure every, Offset, Also at (at most 7). Offsets count from 01:00 and every terminal is set as a loop, so each value is (real departure − 61) mod interval; the real clock minute is under every value. A row with Every 0 has no timetable: the bus leaves on arrival.

Core points and knots. Each line was read over the whole operating day (5:00–23:00) at its core points: terminals, knots where variants split, shared hubs. A trip counts at the time it passes a core point, wherever it starts. Where a slot is defined at a knot, the terminal departure is the knot time minus the running time, and the knot gets no timetable.

Variants. Each destination, path or shift variant is its own Simutrans line on a longer interval (the save game's Ex4). Shift lines repeat every 480 (8 h), the longest interval allowed. Trips more than 3 min off every slot of the line are off the pattern and shown in orange.

Buses needed. One bus followed round the line's own slots with the real running times and at least 3 min at each terminal (less where the slots chain closer, as the real timetable turns). Real buses swap lines at Frýdek-Místek, Podlesí, Karviná and Lutyně all afternoon, so per-line totals are higher than what Transdev runs; pooling lines would be the owner's choice and is not done.

How this was built

Scope. Transdev Slezsko licences with the area prefixes 912 (Havířovsko I and II), 873 (Karvinsko) and 874 (Orlovsko) in the CIS JŘ bus export (CIS JŘ bus JDF export of 20 Oct 2026 (stored by the Havířov/Karviná/Orlová pipeline)), evaluated for Tuesday 20 Oct 2026 with the shared version rule. The prefixes match the live map (provozovna Havířov, Karviná, Orlová) and the register notes (provozní oblast Karvinsko …). 53 ODIS lines; 29 are modelled here, 6 are on other sheets, the rest are listed with the reason.

Method. pipeline/bus-schedule-method.md (v3, the owner's regional rules of 20 Oct 2026): whole-day reading at core points, repeating interval, Ex4 variants, timing at terminals worked back from knots, no daily lines (at most every 480), every afternoon stop on a line, buses from the pattern, every line checked by hand. Each line's choices are in config.json → lines and in the README.

Buses. The ODIS live map (mpvnet.cz) polled every minute 13:05–19:30 on Tuesday 20 Oct 2026 by the Havířov/Karviná/Orlová pipeline gives every bus's trips (licence and trip number), joined to fleet numbers by number plate (seznam-autobusu.cz register). A bus fills one Simutrans line it ran in that line's window, by matching. Buses already on another sheet (Ostrava Bus Vypravenost, section All lines at Hranečník; Havířov Karviná Orlová Bus Vypravenost (town lines)) are not used here.

Sources: CIS JŘ bus JDF, ODIS live map, Transdev Slezsko register.
