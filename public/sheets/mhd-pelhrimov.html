How to read the line cards

Version 2, 23 Nov 2026: every VDV line from Pelhřimov added; the buses come in version 3. The VDV live map keeps no history, so it is polled all day on Tuesday 24 Nov; version 3 names the real bus on every Simutrans line. The lines, typed values and bus counts here stand unless that day shows buses pairing lines in a way the timetable hides.

Typing the timetable. Values go into the Scheduling tab of the terminal entry, with the calendar on: Departure every, Offset, Also at (at most 7). Offsets count from 01:00 and every terminal is set as a loop, so each value is (real departure − 61) mod interval; the real clock minute is under every value. A row with Every 0 has no timetable: the bus leaves on arrival.

Core points and knots. Each line was read over the whole operating day (5:00–23:00) at its core points: terminals, knots where variants split, shared hubs. A trip counts at the time it passes a core point, wherever it starts. Where a slot is defined at a knot, the terminal departure is the knot time minus the running time, and the knot gets no timetable.

Variants. Each destination, path or shift variant is its own Simutrans line on a longer interval (the save game's Ex4). Shift lines repeat every 480 (8 h), the longest interval allowed. Trips more than 3 min off every slot of the line are off the pattern and shown in orange.

Buses needed. One bus followed round the line's own slots with the real running times and at least 3 min at each terminal (less where the slots chain closer, as the real timetable turns). Real buses swap lines at the Pelhřimov bus station, Počátky and Humpolec all afternoon, so per-line totals are higher than what the operators run; pooling lines would be the owner's choice and is not done.

                                                            Scope. MHD Pelhřimov licences 355001–355004 (ICOM transport a.s., provozovna Pelhřimov) and every regional licence of ČSAD AUTOBUSY České Budějovice with a stop in Pelhřimov (VDV areas Humpolecko 357xxx and Pelhřimovsko 358xxx, and the Jindřichův Hradec line 340905, numbered 905 here). Not here: 401 (358401, a PID line, on the PID Benešovsko sheet) and the long-distance lines to Praha (350410, 350804; 4 trips a day each), which are not regional in the CIS JŘ bus export (CIS JŘ bus JDF export of 23 Nov 2026), evaluated for Tuesday 24 Nov 2026 with the shared version rule. 21 lines; 19 are modelled here, the rest are listed with the reason.

                                                            Method. pipeline/bus-schedule-method.md (v4, the owner's rules for regional lines and small towns): whole-day reading at core points, repeating interval, Ex4 variants, timing at terminals worked back from knots, no daily lines (at most every 480), every afternoon stop on a line, buses from the pattern, every line checked by hand. Each line's choices are in config.json → lines and in the README.

                                                            Buses. The VDV live map (mapavdv.kr-vysocina.cz, Kraj Vysočina) polled every minute on Tuesday 24 Nov 2026 gives every bus's licence and trip number (the JDF trip) and its VDV vehicle number; the ČSAD buses also show their number plate, which joins them to the seznam-autobusu.cz register (read on 23 Nov 2026 in the owner's browser). The register lists no fleet numbers for ICOM or ČSAD, so a bus is named by its VDV vehicle number with its plate; a town bus the map shows without a plate keeps its VDV number and its type stays unknown. A bus fills one Simutrans line it ran in that line's window, by matching.
