Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entries listed for each Simutrans line. Units are calendar minutes; the offset counts from 01:00, as the fork does: minutes after 01:00 modulo the interval (the same as after midnight for every interval that divides 60). Leave up to stays automatic (half the gap). Every terminus here is a turning loop or a turn around a block, so every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure, letting the trolleybus clear the loop on time. Offset and each Also at value are the real minute − 1 (mod the interval; the interval is unchanged), with the real minutes shown underneath. This follows the Brno and Prague tram sheets and the Prague trolleybus sheet: a block turn counts as a loop. Put the timetable on the entry the trolleybus leaves from. Branches are separate lines, because Also at cannot change the route. The two DPMB pools (25 + 26, and 35 + 38 + 39) are one Simutrans line each, running the whole circuit, with some stops entered twice. Each Simutrans line is fitted at one rate: every entry sends the same number of vehicles an hour, or they would pile up at one end. Depot runs are not modelled.

Surplus vehicles are marked not needed (dimmed at the bottom of each card): where more were logged than the line needs, exactly the need stays counted, chosen so that every vehicle type of the line stays represented, and afternoon-only kurzy (then the highest kurz numbers) are dropped first. Not-needed vehicles are left out of every count, the depots and the pak totals (user rule, 29 Sep). Vehicles by timetable cycle: follow a vehicle leaving the first entry around 16:00 through every leg of its line, taking at each terminus the first departure at least 3 min after arriving, or less where the timetable itself turns faster (at Černého, Barvičova, Preslova and Pálavské náměstí the arrival and the next departure fall in the same minute). The empty run from Kamenný vrch to Nemocnice Bohunice is taken as 5 min. The 25 + 26 circuit lasts over six hours, longer than the peak, so it is followed on the peak pattern repeated every 20 min. Where more vehicles were out than the need, the surplus is marked not needed (above).

Method and sources

Which day. The network is the timetable in force from Tue 29 Sep 2026. Vehicles are from Fri 25 Sep, the last weekday before it (28 Sep was a public holiday), as on the Brno tram sheet. The timetable did not change between the two days.

Vehicles. BMHD.cz vypravenost for Fri 25 Sep 2026, one page per line (line 25). Ráno, ranní kurz and pouze ranní služba mark morning-only duties. Odpoledne and odpolední kurz mark the afternoon car, and ráno i odpoledne a split duty. The row class tells a trolleybus from a bus. Thu 24 and Tue 29 Sep have the same number of records per line, so the gaps in kurz numbering are DPMB’s, not missing records.

Pools and splits. 26 has no records of its own on weekdays: 25 kurzy work it. Likewise 35 and 38 are worked by 39 kurzy (bmhd.cz “Kurzy a nasazení”; jrbrno.cz combined 25 + 26 timetable). Lines 30 and 37 have two branches each, and which kurz ran which branch is not recorded, so the split is a stated choice sized by the timetable cycle.

Types and depots. BMHD fleet register (evidence DPMB), exact type, depot and livery as read on Tue 29 Sep 2026. Lengths: 12 m for Škoda 26 Tr, 32 Tr and SOR TNS 12; 18 m for the articulated Škoda 27 Tr and 31 Tr.

Timetable. IDS JMK GTFS (kordis-jmk.cz/gtfs/gtfs.zip, exported 25 Sep, stored Mon 28 Sep 2026), trolleybus routes (route_type 800), service day Fri 2 Oct 2026. The feed has no vehicle blocks, so the 25 + 26 and 35 + 38 + 39 circuits come from matching arrivals and departures at each terminus. Line 33 sends 9 an hour out and brings 6 back, and the feed has no trip for the other 3.

Simutrans schedule. Fitted to the afternoon peak in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields, using the shared rule in pipeline/README.md (core 15:30–16:30, longest exact run, simplest pattern), at one rate per Simutrans line. Every terminus is treated as a loop (−1 min), block turns included.

Battery sections. dpmb.cz (21, since 18 May 2026) and Wikipedia’s list of Brno trolleybus lines (30, since 27 Apr 2024). The rest of the network is under wire.

Rebuild. This sheet is generated by pipeline/brno-trolleybus (state “2026-09-29”), which imports the tram pipeline’s helpers from pipeline/brno; see pipeline/brno-trolleybus/README.md.
