MD-CR · Rychlíky Praha – Brno and Leo Express

Simplified Simutrans design for the ČD rychlíky R9 (via Havlíčkův Brod) and R19 (via Česká Třebová) between Praha and Brno, with the R9 Havlíčkův Brod – Brno branch, plus the Leo Express lines. Real JR2026 times (CIS JŘ, every train Mon–Fri 5–9 Oct 2026). The R lines start and end at the odstavné nádraží at Praha and Brno: at Praha on the real times via Praha-Vršovice, at Brno 15 min from the depot to the hl. n. departure and from the hl. n. arrival to the depot, dwell included.

Notation: L = odd hours, S = even hours, (x) = request stop. Highlighted rows are stops with a stay of 5 min or more; they need a timetable entry so the train keeps time. "Leave up to" is 30 min on every entry.

Offsets: Offsets count from 01:00, odd hours first (confirmed 3 Oct 2026): offset = (real time − 60) mod interval. At interval 120, L:08 → 8 and S:08 → 68. At interval 240, offset 0 means 1:00, 5:00, 9:00, 13:00, 17:00 and 21:00, so 8:08 → 188 and 6:08 → 68. The same rule applies at interval 480 (offset 0 = 1:00, 9:00, 17:00).

Vehicles come from each line's full round trip. A vehicle waits at the terminus for the line's next departure, so the round trip is a whole number of intervals (or days for the daily lines).

How the R lines work between Praha and Brno

  • Both R lines start and end at an odstavné nádraží. At Praha the real trains run Praha Jih (depot) – Praha-Vršovice (first and last passenger stop) – Praha hl. n., all on real times. At Brno, where the real trains start and end at Brno hl. n., the depot departure is 15 min before the hl. n. departure and the depot is reached 15 min after the hl. n. arrival (dwell and run together).
  • R9 (via Kolín, Havlíčkův Brod, Žďár nad Sázavou) leaves Praha hl. n. every 2 h at S:04 and Brno hl. n. at S:13.
  • Timed R9 stops: Praha-Vršovice and Brno hl. n. (the real termini, both ways), Praha hl. n., Kolín (where R9 leaves the main line), Havlíčkův Brod (the junction and longest stop, 3–4 min), Tišnov (3 min both ways) and, going south, Brno-Královo Pole (the stop before Brno hl. n.). R19: Praha-Vršovice and Brno hl. n. (both ways), Praha hl. n., Svitavy (5–6 min) and, going south, Blansko.
  • R9 Havlíčkův Brod – Brno fills the hours between them every 4 h: Brno hl. n. L:13, Havlíčkův Brod L:01. So between Havlíčkův Brod and Brno R9 runs every hour in the hours when both lines run, as it does in the real peaks.
  • R19 (via Kolín, Pardubice, Česká Třebová, Svitavy) leaves Praha hl. n. every 2 h at L:58 and Brno hl. n. at S:56.
  • On Praha – Kolín the R lines run with Ex1, Ex2, Ex3, SC, RegioJet and Leo Express; on Pardubice – Česká Třebová – Brno R19 runs with Ex3 (Ex3 S:37 and L:37 from Praha), so R19 leaves Praha 21 min after Ex3 Budapest and arrives in Brno 47 min after it in the same hour.
