Notation: L = odd hours, S = even hours. "Offset" in the fork: odd hours come first, so offset 4 means every 2 h at L:04, and even hours add 60 (offset 66 means S:06). On 212 Havlíčkův Brod (every 60) the offset is simply the minute of every hour. The 210 lines run every 240: offset = (time − 60) mod 240, so 14:15 is offset 75; "Also at" adds more departures in the same 4 hours. (x) = request stop. Highlighted rows are timed stops with a longer stay, which the train needs to keep to time. "Leave up to" is 30 min on every entry of the lines every 120 and 240, and automatic on the line every 60.
