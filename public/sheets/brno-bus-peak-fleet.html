Simutrans schedule

Values to type into the fork’s Scheduling tab, on the stop entries listed for each Simutrans line. Units are calendar minutes, the offset counts from 01:00, as the fork does: minutes after 01:00 modulo the interval (the same as after midnight for every interval that divides 60), and Leave up to stays automatic. Where the bus turns (a loop, turning point or terminus), every departure is set 1 min before the timetable departure so it leaves the turning point on time; offset and Also at values are the real minute − 1, with the real minutes underneath. Through stops (timing points the bus passes without turning, such as Úzká on 40 or Haškova on the Lesná loop) keep the real minute. One-way peak lines (E50, E75, 77 from Areál Slatina) have real departures at their origin only; the far end gets Departure every 0 (no timetable), so the bus heads back at once, as it does out of service in reality. Branches are separate lines, and 57 + 66 around Lesná is one circuit line. Each Simutrans line is fitted at one rate so that no end sends more buses than the other.

Method and sources

Which day. Network from Tue 29 Sep 2026; buses from Fri 25 Sep, the last weekday before it (28 Sep was a holiday), as on the tram and trolleybus sheets. The bus timetable did not change between the two days.

Buses. BMHD.cz vypravenost for Fri 25 Sep 2026, one page per line (line 52). Ráno, ranní kurz and pouze ranní služba mark morning-only duties; odpoledne and odpolední kurz the afternoon bus; ráno i odpoledne a split duty. Buses of other operators are identified on seznam-autobusu.cz.

Types and depots. BMHD fleet register (evidence DPMB), exact type, garage and livery as read on Tue 29 Sep 2026.

Timetable. IDS JMK GTFS (exported 25 Sep, stored Mon 28 Sep 2026), bus routes (route_type 3), service day Tue 29 Sep 2026. The feed has no vehicle blocks and splits long workings at timing points, so each Simutrans line’s order of legs comes from chaining trips at each stop (pipeline/brno-bus/scripts/propose.py), and its size from the timetable cycle.

Simutrans schedule. Fitted to the afternoon peak (core 15:30–16:30) in whole calendar minutes with the fork’s Departure every / Offset / Also at fields, one rate per line (shared rule in pipeline/README.md).
