Independent line-by-line check of `lines.json` against the real trips (`data/derived/reading/<L>.txt`), the builder's
`schedule.json` (slots, typed values, running times, needs, waits), the three audits, the coverage data
(`_coverage.by`, `../../data/derived/stop_okres.json`, `stop_lines.json`) and the neighbouring sheets' `lines.json`
(Praha-západ 311/632, Kladensko 630), with the owner's lessons (`kb/lessons.md`) and the bus schedule method v4 as the
yardstick. The designer's `method-check-2026-11-01.md` was read and every claim in it was checked against the data.

**Result: 29 lines — 23 PASS, 5 FIX, 1 ASK OWNER** (545, which also carries a cost-neutral split fix). Simutrans lines
38 → 39, buses 66 → 64 after the fixes (548 rebuilt as the Ex4 pair −1; 636 Hýskov dropped at the `--final` pass −1;
639 and 545 split at the same cost). Owner checks 3 → 1
(the one sheet-wide near-stop check: Zaječov, u hřbitova and Mezouň, Letník). PID Haló: no Haló line in Berounsko's
`line_class.json`, so the 2 Nov Haló rule has nothing to act on here.

What was verified for every modelled line: the real afternoon departures per direction at the terminal or knot against
the slots (every window slot is a real departure, exact or within 1 min, with the three exceptions named below where a
slot stands for a short working that the game bus runs beyond); interval divides 1440 and is at most 480 (30, 48, 60,
120, 180, 240, 480 used), at most 8 slots, no evenly spaced multi-slot entry (545's 15:40/16:35 and 825's :10/:40/:10 are
uneven and stay as typed); both ends at the same rate, circuits balanced; **all 81 typed values recomputed as
(real − 61) mod every and matching `schedule.json`** (every terminus a loop); the 12 hand-set anchors against the
line's own day; the branch and path audits; which stops each Simutrans line is the only service of; and the 0–5 min
chain tests. Settled points (brief, point 12) were not re-opened: the 0–2-minute real turns with `lay: 0` (526, 529, 531,
544 Hořovice, 547, 548, 631, 639 Lštěň, 643), every-480 lines for a place's only service, the outstation reading of 394
