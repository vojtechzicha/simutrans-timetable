Battery sections

Stretches that DPO runs off the wire on traction batteries, with the lines and trips that use them. Only battery trolleybuses can work these trips: Škoda 26Tr 3731–3740 and every Škoda 36Tr. Lines

Who is on which line. {{WHO}}

Branches and counts. {{BRANCHES_WHY}} Every Simutrans line shows how many trolleybuses were seen and how many it needs. “Needed” follows one bus round the branch at 16:00, with 3 min layovers or the terminus's own shorter timetabled turnaround. A round trip that would wait over 30 min for a return trip is skipped, because by then the buses leave as another line. “Minimum by runtime” is the two running times divided by the headway. Interlining: {{POOLS}}

Headway and runtime come from the timetable for 15:00–17:00. “Leaves 16:xx” lists that terminus's departure minutes in the 16 o'clock hour.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. “Departure every 20, Offset 3, Also at 13” gives :03 :13 :23 :33 :43 :53. Each row is the longest stretch of the afternoon around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (shown under the entry as “exact from–to”). Among stretches of similar length, the simplest pattern wins, and intervals that divide 60 come first. “Leave up to” stays on automatic, which is half the gap.

Loop departures one minute early. Every DPO trolleybus terminus is a turning loop (smyčka), so each Offset and “Also at” value is set 1 min before the timetable departure, so the bus clears the loop on time. That includes the two battery termini, Radvanice, Ludvíkova and Bartovice, Ještěrka. The timetable minutes are listed under each entry for reference, and the “Leaves 16:xx” column shows the real timetable. Where a loop has a separate all-off arrival stop and a boarding stop, put the timetable on the boarding (departure) entry. Other timing points keep their real minutes.

How this was built

The roster is observed. DPO's live feed (dashboard.dpo.cz, vehicleType “troll”) was polled every minute from 16:29 on {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}}, a normal school weekday. Each trolleybus's line and course come from its first sighting between {{WIN}}. That is {{NOBS}} trolleybuses, against a timetable need of {{NEED}}. The feed has no history, which is why the vehicle day is today rather than Friday 25 Sep, the tram sheet's day. The timetable versions are the same on both days (valid 1 Sep – 12 Dec 2026), and its demand is flat from 15:00 to 17:00, so the count at 16:30 matches the need at 16:00. The sheet therefore labels the peak 16:30, the moment actually observed.

Why not the Friday records. seznam-autobusu.cz (fed by tram-bus.cz) holds {{NREC25}} DPO trolleybus records for Fri 25 Sep and {{NREC29}} for today. The records list each bus with its first line and the lines it moves to during the day (“přejezd na linky 101 a 107”), but not the time. That is not enough to say where a bus was at 16:00. They are shown in the depot table as a cross-check.

How many per line. CIS JŘ timetable ({{JDF_LABEL}}, DPO lines 915101–915109; the night lines 111, 112 and 113 are left out), evaluated for {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}}. Each Simutrans line is sized by its own round trip at 16:00 (cycle_need, shared with the tram sheets). {{ADDTXT}}

pak128 objects. Each trolleybus is matched to its object in VZ-DPOstrava-trolleybus. The battery 26Tr (3731–3740) use the 26TrSolaris_partial object, and the others use 26TrSolaris. The 36Tr object is a battery object. The pak has the 26Tr, 27Tr and 36Tr only in the turquoise livery (dpotyrkysova) and the Trollino 12 only in blue-yellow (dpomodrozluta). So the older blue-white-yellow 26Tr and 27Tr, and the advertising wraps, show in turquoise; the Pak128 objects table lists each case.

Simutrans schedule. The departure times entered at loop termini (smyčky) are 1 min earlier than the timetable, as the model requires. The real times are listed under each entry.
