Prague Trolleybus Peak Fleet

The Prague trolleybus network as of Sunday 27 September 2026 (PID timetable of Tuesday 29 September). It lists every trolleybus at the afternoon peak by line and by garage, with the battery (off-wire) sections of each route, the Simutrans schedule for every terminus, and the matching objects of the custom Czech pak128 set. Vehicles are those that ran on Friday 25 September (seznam-autobusu.cz deployment records). Companion to the Prague Tram Peak Fleet sheet, built the same way.

Network at the peak

Every trolleybus line with a peak service. All of them ran with trolleybuses only: no bus was recorded on a trolleybus line on 25 September. Off-wire is the share of the route run on battery (in-motion charging), taken from the GTFS route shape against the OpenStreetMap overhead wire. Each line card names the stretches.

One trolleybus means one vehicle on one course. When a vehicle was replaced during the day, both appear on the course, but only the replacement is counted. A bus on a trolleybus line would go in the “Bus” column, never in the trolleybus classes.

Pak128 objects

The one place with full object names from the custom Czech pak128 set (VZ-DPPraha-trolleybus.pak). Elsewhere each vehicle shows only the core of the name. An articulated trolleybus is several objects that the depot builds as one vehicle, in the order shown. Length is in carunits (16 = one tile). All the objects are road vehicles with an electric engine. The game does not need an overhead wire, so the battery sections are for information only.

Lines

One row per course (kurz) with the vehicle that ran it in the afternoon. Each vehicle shows three lines: number · garage, full type · length, and the pak128 core. Routes list the patterns of the afternoon peak with their 16:00–17:00 headway and runtime. The count under the route is the number of trolleybuses the line needs at 16:00 (runtime both ways plus turnaround).

How this was built

  • Network and day. Chosen the same way as for the Prague tram sheet. The network is the one in force on Sunday 27 September 2026. The timetable is PID's for Tuesday 29 September, the first weekday on that network (28 September is a public holiday). The vehicles come from the last weekday before it, Friday 25 September.
  • Vehicles. seznam-autobusu.cz, vypravenost/pid/vypis?datum=2026-09-25&linka=N for lines 51, 52, 53, 58, 59 and 275: %%N_RECORDS%% records, each with a course, a vehicle and a note. “náhrada za / nahrazen vozem” records a swap (no time given). Coverage is checked against every day since 14 September. Courses 52/9, 59/6 and 59/14 run on other weekdays but are missing on 25 September. The recorded vehicles still cover the round-trip need on every line, so no trolleybus is designed.
  • Garages and types. Every vehicle is matched to its garage, type and paint in the seznam-autobusu.cz register for DPP trolleybuses (trakce=trolejbus). The register has %%N_REGISTER%% vehicles: Klíčov (SOR TNS 18 for line 58), Řepy (Škoda-Solaris 24m, SOR TNS 12, Bozankaya SNG 12T and Škoda 36Tr) and the Střešovice museum.
  • Trolleybuses needed. Follow a trolleybus leaving around 16:00 round the line, taking the first departure at least 3 minutes after each arrival. The departures in that round trip are the number needed. Branches are counted separately. Line 51 uses the timetable's own 2-minute turnaround at Hradčanská.
  • Branches. Lines 52 and 58 each have two branches that run all week, and the course numbers do not say which course runs which. Each branch is its own Simutrans line, because Also at cannot change the route, and each is sized by its round trip. The courses are split in course order as a stated choice. The line card also gives how many trolleybuses a single line alternating the branches would need.
  • Simutrans schedule. The values go on the Scheduling tab of your fork (calendar on). The fit follows the shared rule in pipeline/README.md: the longest exact run of one Departure every / Offset / Also at pattern, in whole minutes, with at most 8 slots. The core is the 16:00 reference hour, not 15:30–16:30: line 58's inbound 12-minute pattern starts at 16:20, and the earlier window gave a 40-minute pattern fitted to only four departures. Lines 51 and 53 run one trolleybus each with irregular gaps, so they get an hourly pattern with its largest deviation shown. Every terminus is set 1 minute early. A trolleybus cannot reverse. It arrives at one stand, turns by a loop, a bus-station circuit or a block, and leaves from another stand; block loops count as loops, as in the Prague tram sheet. Offset and Also at are the timetable minute minus 1 (modulo the interval), with the real minute printed under each. Put the values on the departure stand.
  • Battery sections. Each route's GTFS shape (PID shapes.txt) is sampled every 10 m and compared with the OpenStreetMap ways tagged trolley_wire=yes (Overpass, data of %%OSM_RAW%%, 25 m tolerance). A stop-to-stop section counts as off-wire when less than a third of it runs under the wire. OpenStreetMap is volunteer data, so a newly built or removed section of wire may be missing. The pak128 objects run with or without wire.
  • Pak128 objects. VZ-DPPraha-trolleybus.pak, pak128 main %%PAK_COMMIT%% (build/VZ-DPPraha-trolleybus in the pak checkout). Every peak type has its own object, in the PID grey-red livery that all the peak vehicles wear.
  • Routes. PID GTFS (data.pid.cz/PID_GTFS.zip, export %%GTFS_RAW%%, route type 11 = trolleybus), service date %%SERVICE_DATE%%. The airport terminus is one loop: trips end at Letiště and start at Terminál 1, so both are merged as Terminál 1.
  • Rebuilding. This page is generated by pipeline/praha-trolleybus (python scripts/run.py --state 2026-09-27) from the raw data stored there. pipeline/praha-trolleybus/README.md explains how to update it when the timetable changes.
