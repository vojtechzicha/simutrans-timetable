## Steps (`scripts/`, stdlib-only Python)

1. `01_fetch.py [--jdf] [--fleet] [--records]` fetches the raw inputs.
2. `01b_live_poll.py <until>` polls the live feed: every minute from 13:00 to 19:30, otherwise every 10 min.
3. `02_timetable.py` finds DPO's lines, picks one version each and classifies them: peak, morning, night, or none on the day.
4. `03_fleet.py` builds the fleet (type rules, depot), the records, and live.json for the vehicle day.
5. `04_roster.py` finds branches automatically, sizes their needs and builds the observed roster and any designed buses. The rules are in its docstring:
   - A terminus pair is its own Simutrans line if it has ≥ 6 trips in 15:00–18:00, a 16:xx departure and no garage end.
   - The branch need is `cycle_need_peak`, with max_wait = max(30, 1.5 × headway).
   - The line need is the larger of the branch sum and the buses on the road (Prague bus rule): each non-depot trip holds a bus until arrival + the cycle layover, the most at once in 16:00–16:59. The difference goes to the main branch (23, 56, 59 …).
   - Sparse lines get at least 1 bus.
6. `05_schedule.py` runs runfit for every terminus. Departures are set 1 min early everywhere, as agreed on 29 Sep for all sheets, block turns included.
7. `06_render.py` renders the pending state or the observed state.

## How to update

1. Start `python scripts/01b_live_poll.py <date>T19:45` before 15:00 on a normal weekday.
2. Set `snapshot` (for new raw data), `vehicle_day`, both labels, `network_state_*` and `live.label` in `config.json`.
3. `python scripts/01_fetch.py` (JDF ~107 MB, registers, records).
4. Re-read the výluky and update `network_state`. Check `depot_stops` and `terminus_alias` if termini changed.
5. After the peak, `python scripts/run.py`. Check that no branch has many designed buses. If one does, the branch matching of live trip names may need a look (`04_roster.py`, step 3).
6. Republish.
