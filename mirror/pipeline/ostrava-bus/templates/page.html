Lines

Branches. {{BRANCHES_WHY}}

Buses needed. “Needed” follows one bus round the branch at 16:00, with 3 min layovers or the terminus's own shorter timetabled turnaround. A round trip that would wait longer than 30 min, or 1.5 headways on a sparse line, for a return trip is skipped, because by then the buses leave as another line. Where a line has extensions or short workings that are not separate Simutrans lines, its main branch is topped up to the number of buses the timetable chains together at 16:00, so their buses are not lost. Lines with no complete round trip around 16:00 use the chained count, and at least one bus.

Headway and runtime come from the timetable for 15:00–17:00. “Leaves 16:xx” lists that terminus's departure minutes in the 16 o'clock hour.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. “Departure every 20, Offset 3, Also at 13” gives :03 :13 :23 :33 :43 :53. Each row is the longest stretch of the afternoon around the peak that one pattern reproduces exactly (shown as “exact from–to”). Bus timetables are often irregular, so many rows use a 60-minute cycle with several “Also at” values, and some are exact for only an hour. Rows marked “not scheduled” are variants with too few trips, or depot runs; their minutes are folded into the main row from the same terminus.

Departures one minute early. Every terminus is set as a loop, including block turns and turning points, as agreed for the tram, trolleybus and bus sheets. Each Offset and “Also at” value is 1 min before the timetable departure, so the bus clears the terminus on time. The real minutes are listed under each entry, and the “Leaves 16:xx” column shows the real timetable.

How this was built

The roster comes from the live feed. DPO's live feed (dashboard.dpo.cz, vehicleType “bus”) is polled every minute over the afternoon of {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}}. Each bus's line and course come from its first sighting between {{WIN}}. The feed keeps no history, and seznam-autobusu.cz has almost no DPO bus records (3 on Fri 25 Sep), so the observation is the only per-vehicle source. {{ROSTERTXT}}

Timetable. CIS JŘ bus export ({{JDF_LABEL}}): every line numbered 915xxx whose operator is DPO, found by scanning the export, {{NALL}} lines in all. Evaluated for {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}} with the shared version rule. {{NLINES}} lines run in the afternoon and are on the sheet. The others are listed under “Not in the afternoon peak”.

Machinery. The same code as the Ostrava tram and trolleybus sheets: the JDF reader, round-trip sizing (cycle_need with the 30-minute guard from the trolleybus sheet) and the schedule fit (runfit).

pak128 objects. Each bus is matched to its object in VZ-DPOstrava-bus. The Urbino objects come in generations: s3 is the third generation, in blue-yellow (dpomodrozluta), and s4 the fourth, in turquoise (dpotyrkysova). Urbino 12 III CNG and Urbino 18 III CNG have their own CNG objects, and Urbino 12 III CNG and Urbino 18 III also come in turquoise for the cars repainted in DPO's blue with white stripe. The Urbino 10 is blue-yellow only. The 24 electric Urbino 12 IV (5008–5031) use their own battery object, SolarisUrbino12_s4_electric, in turquoise. It has been installed since 10 Oct 2026. Real paints the object does not have, and advertising wraps, are listed as nearest in the Pak128 objects table.

Sources: CIS JŘ bus JDF, DPO live dashboard, DPO bus register, DPO electric bus register, DPO výluky.
