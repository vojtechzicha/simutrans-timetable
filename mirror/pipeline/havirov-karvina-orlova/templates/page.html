How to read the line cards

Branches. {{BRANCHES_WHY}}

Buses needed. “Needed” follows one bus round the Simutrans line on the schedule this sheet suggests, with the real running times and at least 3 min at each terminus (or the terminus's own shorter timetabled turnaround). A circular line (Karviná 511, 512, 516, 518, 519) is one entry, round the loop and back to its start. Lines without a timed pattern at both ends use the buses the timetable chains at the peak, at least one.

Peak per line. Each line is modelled at its own busiest afternoon stretch: if its busiest 2 hours in 13:00–18:00 have more than one departure more than 15:00–17:00, the schedule, the need and the observation window follow that stretch. Headway and runtime come from the hour before to the hour after that anchor; “Leaves HH:xx” lists the departure minutes in the anchor hour.

The Simutrans schedule holds the values to type into the Scheduling tab of each terminus entry, with the calendar switched on. “Departure every 20, Offset 3, Also at 13” gives :03 :13 :23 :33 :43 :53 (offsets count from 01:00, so every-40, -80, -90 and -120 rows show their first real clock time). Each row is the longest stretch of the afternoon that one pattern reproduces exactly, or an even approximation marked with its worst deviation. Rows marked “not scheduled” are variants with too few trips; their minutes are folded into the main row from the same terminus.

Departures one minute early. Every terminus is set as a loop, as agreed for the tram, trolleybus and bus sheets. Each Offset and “Also at” value is 1 min before the timetable departure. The real minutes are listed under each entry.

Not in the afternoon peak

Lines of the three towns with no regular afternoon service on {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}}: night lines, and lines with fewer than 3 trips in 13:00–19:00 whose stops other lines serve (occasional trips). They are left out of the model. A line as sparse that belongs to another line runs on it instead (503 on 504, 513 and 520 on 514). Where a few trips are a place's only service, they are bent onto a repeating slot of at most 8 hours (no daily lines).

How this was built

The roster comes from the live feed. The ODIS live map (mpvnet.cz, the map behind mapa.kodis.cz) reports every bus with its licence, line, course, trip start and final stop, and number plate. It was polled every minute over the afternoon of {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}} (every 10 min otherwise). Plates are joined to fleet numbers through the seznam-autobusu.cz register. {{ROSTERTXT}}

Timetable. CIS JŘ bus export ({{JDF_LABEL}}): the licences 876401–876421 and 876434 (Havířov), 877511–877520 (Karviná) and 878501–878505 (Orlová), operator Transdev Slezsko (IČO 45192081), {{NALL}} lines in all. Evaluated for {{VEHICLE_DAY_EN}} with the shared version rule (latest valid-from wins). {{NLINES}} lines run in the afternoon and are on the sheet.

Rules. The same code as the Ostrava bus sheet, imported from its pipeline so both follow the owner's rules as they change: each line at its own peak, branches by rule (including regular afternoon branches), both ends of a line at the same rate, branches interleaved at shared termini and junctions, needs sized from the suggested pattern, circuits only where they save a bus, logged surplus marked not needed.

Sources: CIS JŘ bus JDF, ODIS live map, Transdev Slezsko bus register, ODIS vypravenost records, ODIS changes.
