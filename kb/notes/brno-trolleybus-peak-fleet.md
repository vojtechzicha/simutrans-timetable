"Brno Trolleybus Peak Fleet.html" (project root) was built on 2026-09-29, artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/NdYdvPQK1v2sVZ8cGmoW81. It shows Fri 25 Sep vehicles on the network from 29 Sep. The sheet is generated by `pipeline/brno-trolleybus`, which imports the tram helpers and page style from `pipeline/brno`. Its README holds the decisions and the update recipe. See [[city-pipelines]] and [[brno-tram-peak-fleet]].

Non-obvious facts:
- In the IDS JMK GTFS, trolleybuses are route_type 800. The feed has no blocks, so circulations were inferred by balancing arrivals against departures at each terminus.
- DPMB pools: 26 runs on 25 kurzy, and 35 and 38 run on 39 kurzy. bmhd.cz has no weekday records for 26, 35 or 38. Each pool is one Simutrans circuit line.
- Line 33: 9 trips an hour go out to Slatina, sídliště but only 6 come back in the feed, so the sheet adds a designed return slot.
- Battery sections exist only on 21 (Řípská – Letiště) and 30 (beyond Královo Pole, nádraží).
- Trolleybus pak objects already exist: VZ-DPBrno-trolleybus has 26Tr, 27Tr(+B), 31Tr(+B), 32Tr and TNS_12, in the local pak checkout.
- Agreed with the Prague (5a) and Ostrava (f5) trolleybus sessions: every trolleybus terminus is treated as a loop (−1 min), block turns included. A bus on a trolleybus kurz gets a "bus" mark and is not counted as a trolleybus.
- The Brno tram session (c6) wasn't running, so the sheet has not been verified by it.
