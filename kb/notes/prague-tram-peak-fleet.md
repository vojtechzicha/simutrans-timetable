User rules given 2026-09-28 (apply to every city):
- **Designed trams:** if the real records have too few trams, design the additions (cars from the line's recent history, free that day), marked "designed, not recorded".
- **Splits:** branches get separate Simutrans lines, sized by `cycle_need` (from pipeline/brno), by course range where the numbers show the branch, otherwise as a stated choice.
- **Shared pools** are OK and preferred for 5/6. Mark them everywhere, and design the timing as one circulation (`pool_need`).
- **Verification:** always ask the Brno session (simutrans-timetable-c6) to verify, and tell the Ostrava session (simutrans-timetable-10) about it.
- **Display:** no abbreviations; every vehicle cell is 3 lines (numbers · depot / full types · length / pak core).
- **Pak sync:** go through simutrans-custom-czech-pak128-77.
- **Loop termini:** −1 min (watch the phrase "no loop").

**Change sheets (13 Oct 2026):** for a network change the user rebuilds in game, they want a *change sheet*, not a fresh roster: keep the trams already in the game, move as few as possible, show what changed per line, new lines in full, only the trams that must change visible, everything else folded. The artifact now shows this change sheet; when the closure ends (18 Dec), make the reverse change sheet against 2026-10-12. Brno verification of it is still pending.
