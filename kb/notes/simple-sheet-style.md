On 2026-10-03, while I was building the local-trains artifact in the MD-CR look, the user said: "I don't love MD-CR - keep it simple as you did here in chat".

**Why:** the user reads these as working references. The plain markdown-style tables from the chat were what they liked.

**How to apply:** for new design and timetable pages, use one plain column with simple tables (stations line, timetable entries, round trip, notes) and minimal styling. Don't restyle the existing city sheets unless asked. Related: [[md-cr-rail-sheet]], [[local-rail-062-064]].
